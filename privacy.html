Bussin' privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Bussin' is a single-player game by Aloomii Inc. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No accounts. You don't sign in or give us your name, email or any other details.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. The game contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code and makes no network requests of its own.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Stars, tips, upgrades and settings are saved in a file on your phone or tablet. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Purchases. The optional "All Restaurants" purchase is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only asks the store whether the purchase has been made so it can unlock the restaurants and restore them on a new device.
  • Children. The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
