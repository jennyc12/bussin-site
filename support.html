Support

Need help with Bussin'? Email jenny@aloomii.com and we'll get back to you.

  • Bought "All Restaurants" but they're still locked? Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.
  • Restaurants open in order. Even with the purchase, each restaurant opens once you've earned a star on the previous one's level 10.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it, but the purchase can always be restored.
