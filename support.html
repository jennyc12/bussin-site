Support

Need help with Bussin'? Email jenny@aloomii.com and we'll get back to you.

  • iPhone and iPad: All ten Greasy Spoon levels are free. Earn a star to unlock the next level and beat level 10 to unlock Spicy mode. There are no purchases to restore.
  • Android: If you bought "All Restaurants", open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed into the Google account you used to buy it. Restaurants still open in story order as you play.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
